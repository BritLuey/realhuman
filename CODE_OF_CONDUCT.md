# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone participating in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other channel) is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be welcoming, respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the community and the project's users.
- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not
  acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email the maintainers at
**conduct@your-org.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers who don't follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may face consequences determined by the
other members of the project's leadership.
